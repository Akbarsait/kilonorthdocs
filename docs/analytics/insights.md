# Insights Dashboard User Guide

The **Insights Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of your charging habits, energy consumption, and financial trends. It transforms your raw charging data into actionable intelligence.

## 📊 Core Visuals

### Consumption Intensity
- **Chart Type**: Comparative Bar Chart.
- **What it shows**: Monthly total energy usage in kWh.
- **How to read**: The most recent month is highlighted in a vibrant indigo gradient. Older months are shown in semi-transparent white for context.

### Financial Trajectory
- **Chart Type**: Smooth Area Chart (Spend Curve).
- **What it shows**: Total cost trends in CAD over the last 6 months.
- **How to read**: The green area represents your spending pattern. Interactive dots allow you to pinpoint exact costs for specific months.

### 3. Community Intelligence (Phase 2) 💎
KiloNorth thrives on real-time driver data.
- **Submit Intelligence**: Use the "+ Add Insight" button to report station status.
- **Geo-Verification**: To prevent spam, you must be within **500m** of a station to submit a report.
- **Real-time Feed**: See the last 2 hours of verified reports directly in the station sheet.

### 4. Station Bookmarking ⚡️
Save your frequent stops for quick access:
- **Favorite Toggle**: Tap the **Star icon** in the header of any station sheet.
- **Global Sync**: Favorites are saved to your profile and highlighted on the map.
- **Micro-interactions**: Enjoy haptic-style feedback and toast confirmations for all bookmarking actions.

### 📉 Market Snapshot (Primary Row)
- **Visual**: A full-width card featuring the `TrendingUp` financial icon.
- **Data**: Compares **Regional Average** vs. **Your Efficiency**.
- **Context**: Helps you understand if you are paying too much for energy relative to the regional baseline.

## 💡 Premium Interaction Tips

### Surgical Layout
The dashboard uses a **Row 1 / Row 2 hierarchy**:
- **Row 1**: High-level market context (Market Snapshot).
- **Row 2**: Environmental impact (Carbon Offset) and milestones (Century Club) side-by-side.

### Zero-Border Experience
The charts are optimized for a "Noir" experience. Clicking or tapping on bars and points will trigger informative tooltips **without** distracting focus borders or outlines.

### Glassmorphic Tooltips
- **Metric Cards**: Hover or tap on the "Avg Energy" or "Avg Cost" cards to reveal detailed glassmorphic tooltips.
- **Positioning**: Tooltips appear **below** the cards to ensure they never block the navigation header.
- **Rich Context**: Tooltips explain the data source and offer quick comparisons (e.g., "vs last month" performance).

## 🚀 Data-Driven Pro-Tips
At the bottom of the page, the dashboard analyzes your data to provide personalized advice:
- **Efficiency Pro-Tip**: Encourages the 20%-80% SoC best practice.
- **High Cost Detected**: Warns you if your average cost per kWh exceeds local averages and suggests off-peak charging.
- **Heavy Usage Pattern**: Tips for improving charging speeds during high-volume periods.
- **Power User Streak**: Suggests network memberships for frequent chargers to reduce costs.
