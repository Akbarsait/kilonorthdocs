# User Guide: Charging History & Insights

View your charging journey and gain insights into your EV ownership costs.

## 1. Accessing Your History
1.  Click on your **User Profile/Email** in the top right header.
2.  Select **"Charging History"** from the dropdown menu.
3.  You will be taken to your personal history dashboard.

## 2. Understanding Your Stats
At the top of the history page, you'll see two **Compact Summary Cards**:
- **Total Energy**: Your cumulative charging volume in kWh.
- **Total Cost**: Your total spending in CAD.
- *Note: These cards feature a compact inline layout with synced icons and titles for maximum readability.*

## 3. Managing Your Logs
Your sessions are automatically grouped by **Month** to help you track trends:
- **High-Contrast Month Headers**: Clearly marked groups with bold emerald icons.
- **"Day-Only" Calendar Blocks**: Each card features a strictly fixed 44x44px day indicator. This simplified view focuses on the "When" while the header handles the "Month/Year".
- **Session Details**: View location, duration, energy consumed, and cost metrics in nested, high-contrast badges.
- **Actions**:
    - **Edit**: Click the **Pencil** icon to modify a previous session's details.
    - **Delete**: Click the **Trash** icon (high-visibility red) to permanently remove a session. This instantly updates your summary stats!

## 4. Theme Continuity
KiloNorth supports **Smooth Theme Transitions**. Switching between light and dark modes features a graceful liquid-fade effect, ensuring that the history view remains readable and premium in any environment.

---
*Tip: Check your history at the end of the month to see your charging trends compared to previous months!*
