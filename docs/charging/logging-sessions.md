# User Guide: Logging Your Charging Sessions

Logging your sessions in KiloNorth helps you track your energy consumption and costs over time. This guide explains how to use our smart logging tools.

## 1. How to Start a Log
There are two ways to start logging a session:
*   **From History**: Go to **Profile > Charging History** and click the **+ Log Session** button.
*   **From Map**: Click on any station marker. In the station details sheet, click the **Log Session** action button at the bottom.

## 2. Using Vision AI (Recommended)
Tired of typing in numbers from a receipt? Use our Vision AI assistant:
1.  Open the **Log Session** modal.
2.  Click the **"Scan Now"** button in the Vision AI banner at the top.
3.  The system will simulate an AI scan of your receipt and automatically fill in:
    *   Energy added (kWh)
    *   Session duration (Minutes)
    *   Total cost (CAD)
4.  Review the filled fields for accuracy.

## 3. Manual Entry
If you don't have a receipt or prefer manual entry:
1.  Enter the **Station Name** where you charged.
2.  Input the **Energy** consumed in kWh.
3.  Input the **Total Cost** in CAD.
4.  Select the **Date** of the session (defaults to today).
5.  Add any optional **Notes** about the session.

## 4. Saving Your Session
Click the **Save Session** button at the bottom. Once saved, your stats (Total Energy and Total Cost) will be automatically updated in your history dashboard.

---
*Tip: Even if you use Vision AI, you can still edit the fields if the receipt had a typo!*
