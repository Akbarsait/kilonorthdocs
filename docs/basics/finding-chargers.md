# User Guide: Finding Charging Stations

KiloNorth makes it incredibly easy to find the right charging station for your EV. This guide walk you through using the discovery tools on the main map.

## 1. Navigating the Map
*   **Discovery View**: When you open KiloNorth, the map centered on your current location (if permitted) or a major city.
*   **Recenter**: Click the **Navigation Arrow** icon in the bottom right to quickly snap back to your actual location.
*   **Pin Types**:
    *   ⚡ (Single Bolt): Level 2 (Standard) chargers.
    *   ⚡⚡ (Double Bolt): Level 3 (Fast) chargers.
    *   **Colors**: Primary Emerald pins represent charging stations. Selected pins will show a glowing ring.

## 2. Using the Search System
If you are planning a trip or want to look at a specific area:
1.  Click the **Search Bar** at the top of the screen.
2.  Start typing an address, city, or landmark (e.g., "Toronto Eaton Centre").
3.  Select the best match from the results dropdown. The map will smoothly fly to that location.

## 3. Filtering Your Search
Refine your results to see exactly what you need using the **Filter Chips** located below the search bar:
*   **Fast Only**: Toggle this to only see Level 3 (Fast) stations.
*   **High Rated**: Display stations with excellent community feedback.
*   **Available Now**: (Coming soon) Filters for chargers that are currently vacant.
*   **Network**: Filter by specific charging networks (e.g., Flo, IVY).

## 4. Understanding Station Details
Tap on any marker to open the **Station Details Drawer**:
*   **Distance**: See how far the station is from your current position.
*   **Connectors**: View the number and type of plugs available (J1772, CCS, etc.).
*   **Status**: Check if the station is operational or currently under maintenance.

---
*Tip: Keep your eye on the **Near Map View** dashboard in the corner. It gives you a quick summary of how many chargers are available in your immediate view!*
