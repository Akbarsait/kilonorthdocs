# User Guide: Using the Map, Filters, and Map Controls

The interactive map is your primary tool in KiloNorth for discovering and exploring EV charging stations. This guide covers everything you need to know about navigating the map, using filters, customizing your view, and taking advantage of quick-action buttons.

---

## 1. Understanding the Map View

### Starting Location
*   **On First Launch**: The map centers on your current location (if location permission is granted) or defaults to a major city.
*   **Subsequent Sessions**: The map remembers your last position and zoom level.

### Reading Station Markers
*   **⚡ (Single Bolt)**: Level 2 (Standard) charging stations—typically 7-15 kW output.
*   **⚡⚡ (Double Bolt)**: Level 3 (Fast/DC) charging stations—typically 50+ kW output.
*   **Pin Colors**: Stations are displayed in vibrant emerald green. Tap any marker to see detailed information.
*   **Selected Station**: Tapped stations highlight with a glowing ring, making them stand out from others.

### Map Gestures
*   **Pinch to Zoom**: Use two fingers to zoom in and out smoothly.
*   **Two-Finger Rotate**: Rotate the map by twisting two fingers (optional; can be disabled in settings).
*   **Tap to Select**: Tap any station marker to open the **Station Details Drawer** with full information and actions.

## 2. Using Filters to Refine Your Search

Filters help you narrow down the available stations to match your exact needs. The **Filter Chips** appear below the search bar in a horizontal row.

### Available Filters

#### Fast Only
*   **What It Does**: Shows only Level 3 (DC Fast) charging stations.
*   **Use Case**: When you're in a hurry and need a quick charge (15–45 minutes).
*   **Tap to Toggle**: Active filters turn **teal** with a checkmark; inactive filters appear muted.

#### High Rated
*   **What It Does**: Displays only stations with excellent community feedback (typically 4+ stars).
*   **Use Case**: You want reliable, well-maintained chargers based on user experiences.
*   **Community Focus**: Ratings come from real user reviews on the Open Charge Map network.

#### Available Now
*   **What It Does**: Shows only chargers with available plugs (real-time or near real-time availability).
*   **Use Case**: Planning to charge immediately and want to avoid long waits.
*   **Note**: Availability is updated periodically from charging networks.

#### Network Filter
*   **What It Does**: Limits results to specific charging networks (e.g., "Flo", "IVY", "Electrify Canada").
*   **Use Case**: You prefer a particular network for loyalty rewards, app integration, or network coverage.
*   **How to Use**: Tap the **Network** chip to open a dropdown or modal listing available networks in your area.

### Combining Filters
*   **Multiple Active Filters**: All filters work together—e.g., "Fast Only + High Rated" shows only excellent DC fast chargers.
*   **Clear All Filters**: Tap the **Clear** button (if visible) or deselect individual chips to reset your view.

## 3. Floating Action Buttons (FABs) — Quick-Access Controls

FABs are circular button icons floating in the bottom-right corner of the map. Each provides a quick action without opening menus.

### Recenter Button (Navigation Arrow)
*   **Icon**: ↑ (Upward arrow in a circle).
*   **Function**: Instantly returns the map to your current location and re-orients the map northward.
*   **Use Case**: You've scrolled away from your position and want to quickly snap back.
*   **Behavior**: Animates smoothly; does not interrupt other active interactions.

### Favorites Button (Heart Icon)
*   **Icon**: ♡ (Heart outline).
*   **Function**: Opens your saved favorite charging stations.
*   **Use Case**: You've tagged specific stations as favorites and want to quickly return to them without searching.
*   **How to Favorite**: Tap any station and select the heart icon to add it to favorites. Favorites persist across sessions.
*   **Quick View**: Tap this FAB to see all your favorites highlighted on the map or in a list view.

### Layers Button (Map Layers Icon)
*   **Icon**: 🟰 (Stacked layers or grid).
*   **Function**: Opens the **Map Theme Selector** modal to switch between different map styles.
*   **Use Case**: Change the map appearance to suit your preference or lighting conditions.
*   **See Section 4 Below** for detailed information on available themes.

### Additional FABs (If Present)
*   **Expand/Collapse**: Some FABs may combine into a single button that expands to show more options when tapped.
*   **Stacked Layout**: On smaller screens, FABs may stack vertically to save space.

## 4. Switching Map Themes

KiloNorth offers five distinct map themes to suit different preferences, lighting conditions, and use cases.

### Opening the Theme Selector
1. Tap the **Layers FAB** button (stacked layers icon) in the bottom-right corner of the map.
2. The **Map Theme Modal** opens, displaying all available themes with previews.
3. Your currently active theme is highlighted with a checkmark and labeled **"Active"**.

### Available Themes

#### Streets (Default)
*   **Visual Style**: Clean street-level map with POI (point of interest) labels and clear roads.
*   **Best For**: Everyday navigation and finding nearby amenities.
*   **Preview Color**: [Placeholder: Neutral gray and white tones]
*   **Lighting**: Works well in daylight and is readable on phones.

#### Outdoor
*   **Visual Style**: Topographic map with terrain elevation, natural features, and vegetation indicators.
*   **Best For**: Road trips, planning routes through rural areas, and understanding terrain.
*   **Preview Color**: [Placeholder: Green and tan earth tones]
*   **Lighting**: Excellent for day use; slightly muted in low light.

#### Winter
*   **Visual Style**: Seasonal map variant emphasizing snow coverage, winter landmarks, and cold-weather infrastructure.
*   **Best For**: Users in winter climates wanting a thematic, relevant map appearance.
*   **Preview Color**: [Placeholder: Cool white and pale blue tones]
*   **Lighting**: Readable year-round; particularly suited to December–March.

#### Bright
*   **Visual Style**: High-contrast, minimalist light theme with bold typography and accent colors.
*   **Best For**: Daytime use, outdoor viewing, and users who prefer a light theme for accessibility.
*   **Preview Color**: [Placeholder: Stark white background with high-contrast text]
*   **Lighting**: Ideal for outdoor use in strong sunlight; may cause glare indoors.

#### Hybrid
*   **Visual Style**: Satellite imagery as the base layer with street overlays for reference.
*   **Best For**: Users who want to see actual terrain, vegetation, and land usage while still having road labels.
*   **Preview Color**: [Placeholder: Satellite photo with semi-transparent street lines]
*   **Lighting**: Excellent for detailed location context; less suitable for quick navigation.

### How to Switch Themes
1. Open the Theme Selector (Layers FAB).
2. Tap any theme option to instantly preview it on the map.
3. The map updates **immediately**—no need to confirm. Compare themes before deciding.
4. Your choice is **automatically saved** and will be the default next time you open KiloNorth.
5. Tap the **Done** button to close the modal, or continue comparing themes.

### Tips for Theme Selection
*   **Time of Day**: Use "Bright" during daylight, "Streets" or "Outdoor" during evening.
*   **Connectivity Context**: "Hybrid" is great when scouting a new area for the first time.
*   **Accessibility**: "Bright" and "Hybrid" offer higher contrast for users with visual sensitivities.
*   **Performance**: "Streets" theme uses fewer tiles and may load slightly faster on slower connections.

## 5. App Theme (Dark Mode / Light Mode)

Separate from the map theme, KiloNorth's overall interface (modals, buttons, text) also has a dark and light mode.

### Accessing App Theme Settings
1. Tap your **Profile Icon** in the top-left or top-right of the app (location varies by page).
2. Select **Account > Preferences** from the menu.
3. Toggle between **Light** and **Dark** themes using the theme switch.
4. Your preference is saved and applied immediately.

### What Changes with App Theme
*   **UI Colors**: Buttons, backgrounds, text, and modal styles adapt to light or dark.
*   **Map Overlay Contrast**: Modals and floating elements adjust opacity and colors for readability.
*   **Dark Mode Benefit**: Reduces eye strain in low-light environments and saves battery on OLED screens.

### Note
*   **Map Theme vs. App Theme**: These are independent. You can use a "Bright" map theme with a dark app UI, or vice versa.

## 6. Searching for Specific Locations

### Using the Search Bar
1. Tap the **Search Bar** at the top of the map.
2. Type an address, city name, landmark, or station name (e.g., "Toronto Eaton Centre" or "401 Richmond St").
3. Autocomplete suggestions appear as you type.
4. Tap a result to smoothly fly the map to that location.

### Favorites Integration
*   **Search Favorites**: Start typing a favorite station name to quickly jump to it.
*   **Save for Later**: Once at a location, you can favorite it for future visits.

---

## 7. Viewing Station Details

### Opening a Station Details Drawer
1. Tap any station marker on the map.
2. The **Station Details Drawer** slides up from the bottom, showing:
   - **Distance**: How far the station is from your current location.
   - **Charger Types**: Available connectors (e.g., J1772, CCS, Tesla Supercharger).
   - **Network**: Which charging network operates this station.
   - **Status**: Whether the station is operational, under maintenance, or closed.
   - **Address & Phone**: Full location details and contact information.
   - **User Ratings**: Community feedback score (if available).

### Actions from Station Details
*   **Log Session**: Click the **Log Charging Session** button to record a charging event at this station.
*   **Add to Favorites**: Tap the heart icon to save this station.
*   **Get Directions**: Tap the **Directions** button to open your phone's navigation app (Google Maps, Apple Maps).
*   **View More Info**: Tap **Learn More** to see extended details, user reviews, and photos.

### Closing the Drawer
*   Swipe down or tap the **X** button at the top of the drawer.
*   Tap outside the drawer to close it.

## 8. Tips & Tricks

### Navigation Efficiency
*   **Tap to Favorite**: Favorite stations you visit regularly so you can jump to them instantly.
*   **Use Recenter Wisely**: If you've drifted far from your location, hit Recenter to snap back without manually panning.

### Smart Filtering
*   **Combine Filters Strategically**: Use "Fast Only + High Rated" during road trips to find reliable quick chargers only.
*   **Clear Filters When Exploring**: Remove filters to see the full charging network in a new area.

### Theme Switching for Different Conditions
*   **Low Battery on Phone?** Use the "Streets" theme—it's optimized and uses fewer resources.
*   **Can't See Labels?** Switch to "Bright" for maximum contrast in sunlight.
*   **Exploring New Territory?** Use "Hybrid" to see actual terrain and land usage alongside roads.

### Offline Support
*   **Previously Viewed Map Tiles**: Tiles from your session are cached and viewable even without an internet connection.
*   **Favorite Stations**: Saved favorites are available offline; new searches require connectivity.

## 9. Troubleshooting

### Map Not Centering on My Location
*   **Check Permissions**: Go to your phone's Settings > KiloNorth and ensure location permission is enabled.
*   **Try Recenter**: Tap the Recenter FAB to manually trigger a location update.
*   **Refresh App**: Close and reopen KiloNorth.

### Filters Not Showing Results
*   **Too Many Filters Active**: Try disabling one or more filters to see if results appear.
*   **Check Area**: Some rural areas have fewer charging stations. Zoom out or move to a city center.
*   **Network Connectivity**: Ensure you have an active internet connection; filters require live data.

### Theme Not Saving
*   **Confirm Done**: Make sure you tapped the **Done** button after selecting your theme.
*   **Clear App Cache**: On your phone, go to Settings > Apps > KiloNorth and clear the cache.

### Map Appears Blurry or Slow
*   **Internet Speed**: Tiles download progressively. On slow networks, wait a few seconds for tiles to load.
*   **Device Storage**: Ensure your phone has at least 50 MB of free space for tile caching.
*   **Zoom Level**: If very zoomed in or out, zoom to a moderate level (level 12–16) for best performance.
